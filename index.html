Research interests

From process to performance

Processing sets structure, structure sets properties, and properties set performance. My interests follow that chain, and so does the work I've done so far.

  1. STEP 1

    Process

    How is it made?

    • Additive manufacturing
    • Process optimization
    • Intelligent process control
    From my work
    • Built an FDM 3D printer for my senior thesis
    • Prototyped JF-17 ventral fins by directed energy deposition
  2. STEP 2

    Structure

    What forms inside it?

    • Microstructure
    • Metamaterial architecture
    From my work
    • Layer adhesion and porosity in FDM-printed parts
    • Metallography of H13 and 42CrMo4 steels
  3. STEP 3

    Property

    How does it respond to load and heat?

    • Material characterization
    • Mechanical testing
    From my work
    • Tensile, bend, torsion, compression and hardness testing
    • OES chemical analysis and SEM
    • Thermal simulation of steel and aluminum in ANSYS
  4. STEP 4

    Performance

    What can it do in service?

    • 4D-printed metamaterials
    • Component design
    From my work
    • Review of stimuli-responsive 4D-printed metamaterials, in preparation
    • Engine parts: rocker covers, cylinder heads, pistons
    • Stress and fracture at gear-tooth roots and shaft necks
